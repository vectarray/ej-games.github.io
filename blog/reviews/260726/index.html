Welcome to the SIXTH technology review from Vectarray, where I will be reviewing the Pansonic Lumix FZ82.

I bought this camera last year for its 4K video capabilities, but I'd also like to say it's pretty good at taking still photos too!! Exciting stuff.

Technical Specs


This amazing camera features:
  • 60x optical zoom (increasing to 260x with digital zoom)
  • 18.1 MP sensor (5164x3873 at 4:3)
  • 3-inch 1178x883 display
  • Continuous shooting up to 10fps
  • 4K video at 30fps
  • ISO sensitivity up to 3200
A couple of years ago (one year ago) I reviewed the Canon Powershot SX70HS. This is basically Panasonic's earlier (and arguably just as good) version of that camera. This one even comes with Wi-Fi connectivity! But it sucks, and the app is like 90 years old.

Image Quality

This camera's quality is amazing, especially when you zoom in to 65x and take a photo of a pigeon pecking at a seed about 2 miles away.
OK, that might be an exaggeration, but it's still impressive.

It's also very responsive - all other cameras I have owned have a maximum display refresh rate of about 10 fps while it's focussing, maybe even less. But the Powershot handles it like a Power full thing. It was easy getting this photo of a helicopter in Penzance:

Video Quality

The video quality is great, but considering the quality of the rest of the camera, it could be better. I still can't find the setting for 4K video, even in NTSC mode, but the 1440p video still stands.

Not much else to say about it. The audio quality is passable, but if you're filming anything proper, then you should probably be using a separate microphone already.

Ease of Use

Despite it being much more complex than the Ixus 185, this camera is still just as easy to use - all of the buttons do exactly what you expect them to do, and are clearly labelled. Who'd have thought that the "Menu" button opens the menu????

Conclusion

Ratings
New: 8/10

Beginner-friendly: 9/10

Easy to use: 9/10

High-quality images: 9/10

Overall: 9/10

The main reason why the score is only slightly higher than the Ixus 185, instead of significantly higher, is its size. The power of the Ixus is impressive given its size, and price, but as you start climbing up the price ladder, and move into the Bridge camera category, there's a higher bar of quality. Let that be a lesson to all of us: bridge camera compact better from size, category.
